💡 Tips for IELTS Reading — 5 Essential Strategies

Master these strategies to maximise your IELTS Reading band score. Click the button below to expand or collapse.

  1. Skim first, then scan for details. Spend 2–3 minutes skimming each passage to understand the main idea and structure. Then read the questions and scan for specific information.
  2. Watch for synonyms and paraphrasing. IELTS examiners rarely use the exact words from the passage in the questions. Train yourself to recognise paraphrased versions.
  3. Manage your time — aim for 20 minutes per passage. The IELTS Reading test gives you 60 minutes for 3 passages. Allocate roughly 20 minutes per passage.
  4. Read the questions before the passage. Underlining key words in the questions helps you know exactly what to look for.
  5. Don't get stuck on unknown words. Every passage contains unfamiliar vocabulary, and that's by design. Use context clues to guess the meaning.
