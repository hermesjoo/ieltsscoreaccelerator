💡 Tips for IELTS Reading — 5 Essential Strategies

Master these strategies to maximise your IELTS Reading band score. Click the button below to expand or collapse.

  1. Skim first, then scan for details. Spend 2–3 minutes skimming each passage to understand the main idea and structure. Then read the questions and scan for specific information. This saves time and improves accuracy compared to reading every word carefully.
  2. Watch for synonyms and paraphrasing. IELTS examiners rarely use the exact words from the passage in the questions. Train yourself to recognise paraphrased versions — for example, "significant" in a question may correspond to "considerable" or "substantial" in the passage.
  3. Manage your time — aim for 20 minutes per passage. The IELTS Reading test gives you 60 minutes for 3 passages. Allocate roughly 20 minutes per passage, spending more time on harder ones. If a question takes more than 2 minutes, mark your best guess and move on.
  4. Read the questions before the passage. Underlining key words in the questions helps you know exactly what to look for. This targeted approach prevents wasted time on irrelevant information and reduces the chance of misreading.
  5. Don't get stuck on unknown words. Every passage contains unfamiliar vocabulary, and that's by design. Use context clues to guess the meaning. Often, the surrounding sentences provide enough information to answer questions correctly without understanding every single word.

Understanding how your raw score translates to an IELTS band score helps you set realistic targets.

Band 9 71–75 (95–100%) Expert User — Has fully operational command of the language. Appropriate, accurate and fluent with complete understanding.

Band 6.0–6.5 41–52 (55–70%) Competent User — Generally has effective command despite some inaccuracies and misunderstandings.

Note: The actual IELTS band-to-score conversion can vary slightly between test administrations. These figures are approximate guidelines based on recent official conversions.
