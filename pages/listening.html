💡 Tips for IELTS Listening

  1. In the real exam, the audio is played only once. Take the exercises seriously.
  2. Before listening, read the questions in advance so you know what to look for.
  3. Listen for key words (numbers, names, dates) — they are usually the answers.
  4. Check your spelling. Incorrect spelling means no score in fill-in-the-blank.
  5. Don't ignore articles (a, an, the) next to the blanks — they guide the type of answer.
  6. Use the audio playback button to listen to the transcript like the real exam.
  7. Part 1: Focus on names, numbers, and spelling. Score the most here.
  8. Part 2: Pay attention to maps, plans, or diagrams if shown.
  9. Part 3: Listen for opinions and agreements/disagreements.
  10. Part 4: Hardest part — no breaks. Stay focused and read ahead.
  11. Don't get stuck: If you miss an answer, move on immediately.
