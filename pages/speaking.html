Part 1 Introduction & Interview

4-5 minutes — Personal questions about home, work, studies, and interests

1. Where are you from?

I'm originally from Tehran, which is the capital city of Iran. It's a bustling metropolis with a rich history and vibrant culture. I've lived there for most of my life, and I really enjoy the energy of the city, especially the local bazaars and cafés.

2. Do you work or study?

Currently, I'm a university student studying Computer Science. I find it fascinating because it combines logical thinking with creativity. I'm particularly interested in artificial intelligence and how it can be applied to solve real-world problems.

3. What do you like to do in your free time?

In my spare time, I'm quite keen on reading books, especially non-fiction. I also enjoy going for walks in nature as it helps me unwind. Additionally, I've recently taken up cooking, which I find surprisingly therapeutic and rewarding.

4. Do you prefer mornings or evenings? Why?

I'd say I'm more of a morning person. I find that I'm most productive and focused in the early hours, probably because everything is quieter and there are fewer distractions. I usually start my day with a cup of coffee and some reading.

5. What kind of music do you listen to?

I have quite eclectic taste in music. I enjoy everything from classical pieces by composers like Chopin to modern pop and indie rock. When I'm studying, I usually listen to lo-fi beats or jazz as it helps me concentrate.

Part 2 Long Turn (Cue Card)

1 minute preparation + 2 minutes speaking — Describe a specific topic

💡 Strategy: Use the 1 minute preparation wisely! Take notes and structure your answer: What, Where, When, Why, How.

📋 Cue Card 1

Describe a place you would like to visit.

  • Where it is
  • How you learned about it
  • What you would do there
  • Why you want to visit
⏱ 1:00

I'd love to visit Kyoto, Japan, which is renowned for its beautiful temples, traditional gardens, and rich cultural heritage. I first became interested in Kyoto after watching a documentary about Japanese tea ceremonies, and since then, I've been fascinated by the city's blend of ancient traditions and modern life.

If I had the chance to visit, I would spend my time exploring the iconic Fushimi Inari Shrine with its thousands of vermillion torii gates, and I'd love to experience a traditional ryokan stay. I'd also visit the Arashiyama bamboo grove and try authentic Japanese cuisine, particularly matcha desserts and kaiseki dining.

The main reason I want to visit Kyoto is that it seems to offer a unique glimpse into traditional Japanese culture that hasn't been overshadowed by modernization, unlike some other major cities.


📋 Cue Card 2

Describe a skill you would like to learn.

  • What the skill is
  • How you would learn it
  • Who would teach you
  • Why you want to learn it
⏱ 1:00

A skill I'd really like to develop is playing the piano. I've always been drawn to music, and I believe that learning a musical instrument would not only be enjoyable but also beneficial for my cognitive abilities and emotional well-being.

I would probably start by taking lessons from a professional piano teacher at a local music academy. I think having a structured learning environment with regular feedback would be the most effective approach. I'd also supplement this with online tutorials and practice exercises.

The reason I'm particularly interested in the piano is that it's such a versatile instrument. You can play anything from classical sonatas to modern pop songs, and it's a wonderful way to express emotions without words.

Part 3 Discussion

4-5 minutes — More abstract discussion related to the Part 2 topic

1. How has technology changed the way people travel?

Technology has revolutionized travel in numerous ways. For instance, booking flights and accommodation has become incredibly convenient through apps and websites. Navigation is also much easier now with GPS and mapping applications. However, some argue that technology has somewhat diminished the sense of adventure and discovery that travel once offered.

2. Do you think it's important for people to learn new skills throughout their lives?

Absolutely. In today's rapidly evolving job market, continuous learning is essential. People who regularly acquire new skills tend to be more adaptable and competitive. Moreover, learning keeps the mind sharp and can boost self-confidence. I believe that lifelong learning should be encouraged at every stage of life, not just during formal education.

3. Why do some people prefer to travel alone rather than in groups?

There are several reasons for this. Some people value the freedom and flexibility that solo travel provides — they can set their own itinerary without having to compromise. Others might find it easier to immerse themselves in a new culture when traveling alone, as they're more likely to interact with locals. Additionally, solo travel can be a great opportunity for self-reflection and personal growth.

💡 General Speaking Tips
🎯 Band 7+ Strategies:
  • Fluency: Speak without long pauses. Use fillers like "That's an interesting question..." while thinking
  • Vocabulary: Avoid repetition. Instead of "good" say "excellent, outstanding, remarkable"
  • Grammar: Use varied structures — passives, conditionals, different tenses
  • Pronunciation: Focus on word stress and sentence intonation patterns
  • Extend your answers: Don't give short answers. Add explanations, examples, and reasons
  • Don't memorize: Never give memorized answers! Examiners can tell immediately
