💡 نکات کلیدی Task 1

  • هرگز نظر شخصی ندهید — فقط واقعیات را گزارش کنید
  • پاراگراف معرفی (Introduction) را با تغییر لغات بازنویسی کنید
  • یک پاراگراف Overview داشته باشید (مهم‌ترین روندها)

📝 قالب آماده Task 1

Band 8.0 Sample

📈 نمونه توصیف نمودار خطی — مصرف انرژی

The line graph illustrates changes in energy consumption across four different sources in the UK between 2000 and 2020.

Overall, it is evident that renewable energy sources experienced a dramatic rise over the period, while fossil fuel consumption declined steadily. Natural gas remained the most consumed source throughout, though its dominance narrowed considerably.

Beginning at approximately 40%, natural gas consumption peaked at 45% in 2005 before gradually falling to around 30% by 2020. Meanwhile, coal experienced the most significant decline, dropping from 35% in 2000 to just under 10% by the end of the period.

In contrast, renewable energy showed a remarkable upward trajectory. Starting from a mere 5% in 2000, it climbed steadily, surpassing coal by 2015 and reaching approximately 35% by 2020. Solar and wind power together accounted for the bulk of this growth, rising from 2% to 25% over the two decades.

Band 7.0 Sample

📊 نمونه توصیف نمودار ستونی — هزینه تحصیل

The bar chart compares the average annual tuition fees in four countries for the years 2010 and 2020.

Overall, all four countries saw an increase in tuition fees over the decade. The USA consistently had the highest fees, whereas Germany remained the most affordable option.

In 2010, students in the USA paid approximately $20,000 per year, which rose to $30,000 by 2020. The UK followed a similar pattern, increasing from $15,000 to $22,000. Australia saw a more modest rise, going from $12,000 to $18,000. Germany, despite its increase from $500 to $3,000, remained significantly cheaper than the others.

Band 8.0 Sample

نمونه انشا — شبکه‌های اجتماعی (Opinion)

Some people believe that social media has a negative impact on society. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Introduction: In recent years, social media platforms have become an integral part of daily life for billions of people worldwide. While some argue that these platforms have a detrimental effect on society, I largely disagree with this view, as I believe the benefits of social media far outweigh its drawbacks.

Body 1: One of the most compelling arguments in favour of social media is its ability to connect people across geographical boundaries. Platforms such as Instagram and Twitter enable individuals to maintain relationships regardless of distance. Furthermore, social media has proven to be a powerful tool for raising awareness about social issues. Movements like #MeToo gained momentum largely through viral posts, demonstrating their potential for positive societal change.

Body 2: Admittedly, critics raise valid concerns regarding mental health, particularly among young people. Studies suggest a correlation between excessive screen time and increased rates of anxiety. However, I would argue that the issue lies not with the technology itself, but rather with how individuals choose to use it. With proper digital literacy education, these negative effects can be significantly mitigated.

Conclusion: In conclusion, while social media is not without its challenges, I firmly believe that it serves as a net positive for society. The key lies in promoting responsible usage rather than demonising the platforms themselves.

Band 7.5 Sample

نمونه انشا — آموزش آنلاین (Discussion)

Some people think that online learning is better than classroom learning. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

Introduction: The rise of digital technology has transformed the education sector, with online learning becoming increasingly popular. While some believe that virtual classrooms offer superior advantages, others maintain that traditional face-to-face learning remains more effective.

Body 1: Proponents of online learning argue that it offers unparalleled flexibility. Students can access lectures at any time, which is particularly beneficial for those with work commitments. Moreover, online courses often cost less than traditional programmes, making education more accessible.

Body 2: On the other hand, advocates of classroom learning emphasise the importance of direct interaction. The ability to ask questions in real-time and engage in group discussions fosters deeper understanding. Additionally, the structured environment helps many students maintain discipline and focus.

Conclusion: In my opinion, the most effective approach combines both methods. Blended learning can harness the flexibility of digital platforms while preserving the social benefits of in-person education.
